# Required Frames are seeded by pseudo-steps, not the Outcome

IPOCL only creates Frames for Steps on the Outcome's causal chain, so a Character goal the author wants pursued, such as the Protagonist's Desire, has to be pulled into the plan explicitly. Each Required Frame adds a pseudo-step: no Actors, no effects, one precondition (the Character goal), ordered after init and before the goal Step. Whatever establishes that precondition must be the final Step of a matching Frame. We chose this over adding the goal to the Outcome because the Outcome means "holds at the end". A Desire that is achieved and then lost, which tragedies need, would be impossible. The Outcome would also accept the goal being reached through another Character's Frame. Pseudo-steps are the intended building block for author goals and Milestones (`spec.md` §9, ticket 20), so extend this mechanism rather than adding a parallel one.
